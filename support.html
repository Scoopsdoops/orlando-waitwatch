Orlando WaitWatch Support

Need help with Orlando WaitWatch? This page will help you find assistance with the app.

Wait times

Wait times may change quickly and may occasionally be delayed or unavailable. Always follow official park information and posted instructions.

Map and favorites

If the map doesn't load, check your internet connection and try refreshing the app. If favorites aren't appearing, check whether your browser or device is preserving local app data.
